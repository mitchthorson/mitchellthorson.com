This piece was part of a large scale package produced by USA Today and The Arizona Republic that examined the state of the border between the United States and Mexico in light of President Trump's promise to build a wall spanning the border.

My colleague Pim Linders and I were tasked with building an interactive map that visualized the data collected by our colleagues about what existing structures existed along the border. The map also allowed the user to access any point along the georeferenced aerial footage of the region along the entire length of the border shot by my photojournalist colleagues.

The project in whole would go on to win the [2018 Pulitzer Prize in Explanatory Reporting](https://www.pulitzer.org/winners/staffs-arizona-republic-and-usa-today-network).
