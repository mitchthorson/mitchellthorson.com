This piece was part of a large collaborative investigative reporting package by USA Today and The Arizona Republic. My role, along with several other graphics desk colleagues, was to help visualize and summarize the data collected by my colleagues as part of their reporting. We created a scroll-based data narrative that served as a visual introduction to the project. I created several of the visualizations and helped develop the framework for scroll-based stories used here.
