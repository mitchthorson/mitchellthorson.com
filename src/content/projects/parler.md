Reporters Aleszu Bajak and Jessica Guynn reported a story on a sentiment analysis of scraped posts from the social media website Parler concurrent with the events in Washington, DC on January 6, 2021. I was asked to visualize the data from their analysis. The primary visualization is a chart that shows the changing emotional tenor of posts over time, as well as the volume of total posts.
